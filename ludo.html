How to play Ludo

  • You are Red. Green, Yellow, and Blue are computers.
  • Roll a 6 to move a token out of the yard onto the board.
  • Rolling a 6 also gives an extra turn.
  • Land on an opponent (not on a safe star) to send them back home.
  • Enter your colored home path, then reach the center with an exact roll.
  • If several tokens can move, click the one you want.
  • First to get all four tokens home wins.
