How to play

  • Both players start off the board at 0.
  • Roll a die and move that many squares (1–6).
  • Land on a ladder bottom → climb up.
  • Land on a snake head → slide down.
  • You must reach exactly 100 to win (overshoot stays put).
  • You are green; the computer is amber.
